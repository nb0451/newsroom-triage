# Category rules

These are the published rules for the four AG News categories. The assistant
may quote from this file when it explains a decision.

## World
International news, politics, conflict, disasters, national affairs and
diplomacy. A foreign company's earnings or a currency move is Business,
not World, even though it happens abroad.

## Sports
Matches, results, athletes, teams, transfers and tournaments. A broadcasting
rights deal or a club's finances is Business, not Sports, even though it is
about a sports organisation.

## Business
Companies, markets, earnings, the economy, trade, jobs and deals. A technology
company's share price is Business. Its product's technical capability is
Sci/Tech.

## Sci/Tech
Science, research, technology, software, space and health research. A new
chip's speed is Sci/Tech. The money the chipmaker made from it is Business.

## When two fit
Choose the one the item is mostly about, judged by the headline rather than
by one word buried in the description.
